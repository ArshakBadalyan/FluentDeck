How it works

Practice speaking any language with AI in three steps

  1. 1

    Talk with your AI tutor

    Speak out loud or type. Pick a free conversation, a topic or a real-life role-play like a job interview.

  2. 2

FAQ

Questions about practicing a language with AI

Is Riovox free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 5 minutes of AI conversation every day, plus flashcards to review your mistakes. Premium raises that to up to 4 hours a day, removes ads and unlocks all speaking content.

Can I practice speaking out loud?

Yes. Tap the microphone and talk. Your tutor listens, answers in a natural voice and gently corrects your mistakes. You can also type if you prefer.

How is it different from a normal chatbot?

Riovox is built for speaking practice: it adapts to your level (A1 to C2), points out mistakes kindly, remembers your goals, interests and common mistakes, and turns corrections into flashcards you review later.

Which languages can I practice?

Sixteen: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Mandarin, Japanese, Russian, Hindi, Turkish, Korean, Arabic, Vietnamese, Indonesian and Armenian. Set any of them as your own language and practice any other, for example Spanish → English, English → Japanese or Turkish → German.

Can I import my other decks?

Yes. Riovox imports .apkg and text files, and can keep your review progress.

How do I cancel Premium?

Anytime, in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
